## Source quality & data-integrity fixes

1. **Rejected synthetic third-party blog ranges**: During verification, `businessconnect.com.ng/oyo-state-postal-codes/` was caught publishing fabricated sequential `x01–x99` ranges (`200101–219999`) contradicting NIPOST’s real delivery districts (`200001–212104`, `Ogbomoso 210211`, `Oyo Town 211211`). Every state’s codes were anchored to `https://nigeriazipcode.com/<state>/` (NIPOST delivery-district compilation) and cross-checked against `apartments.ng`, `nigeriahousingmarket.com`, and `businessconnect.com.ng` where corroborated.
2. **Eliminated `#sources` `.slice(0, 5)` truncation**: `app/codes/postal/[state]/page.tsx` now renders `getAggregatedStateSources(s)` so 100% of distinct state + place source URLs render on-page.
3. **Fixed duplicate `| NaijaCheck` title suffix** on `/codes/postal/[state]/[area]/page.tsx`.
4. **Upgraded `/codes/postal` UX (`PostalSearch`)**: Added direct matched-place results with 1-tap `CopyButton`, geopolitical zone filter pills, quick-query chips, curated district guide cards (`20`), and an explicit honest coverage notice (*"not every street in Nigeria"*).
